# Privacy Policy for Woodworking Calculator

**Last Updated:** February 15, 2026

## Overview

Woodworking Calculator ("the App") is a tool designed to help woodworkers calculate angles for pyramid construction and reference compound miter angles. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

The App operates entirely on your device and does not:
- Collect personal data
- Track your usage
- Send data to external servers
- Use analytics services
- Require user accounts or registration
- Access your contacts, photos, or other device data

## Data Storage

All calculations and app settings are stored locally on your device only. No data leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
